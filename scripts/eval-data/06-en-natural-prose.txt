The morning sun rose over the quiet valley as the farmers began their daily work. She walked to the market with her brother, carrying a basket of fresh vegetables that they had picked from the garden. "We should sell these before noon," she said, glancing at the gathering clouds. By the time they reached the village square, a small crowd had already formed near the old fountain, and the children were laughing as they chased one another through the narrow streets.
